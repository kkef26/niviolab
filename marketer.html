FAQ

Common questions

What is Nivio Marketer?

Nivio Marketer is an operating system for bulk commodity marketing desks. It plans, contracts, routes, and settles every physical commodity move — crude, refined products, biofuels, NGLs, grains — on one surface with a complete audit trail.

Is Nivio Marketer a trading platform?

No. Nivio Marketer manages the physical movement of bulk commodities — negotiating and executing contracts to move volume from origin to destination. It is not a financial trading or exchange platform.

How does the Nivio Marketer solver work?

It uses a deterministic constraint solver — a Proprietary Algorithm, not a black box — that finds margin-optimal allocations across suppliers, routes, counterparties, and price. Every decision is auditable and explainable.

Does Nivio Marketer replace our ERP?

No. Nivio Marketer is the decision layer for the marketing desk. It works alongside your ERP and TMS and exports settled deals to your accounting system — it does not replace them.
