FAQ

Common questions

What is Nivio Bind?

Nivio Bind is trucking insurance submission software for brokers that returns a carrier risk verdict in under 200ms — a composite A–F score, nuclear-verdict flags, chameleon detection, and litigation history — scored across 800,000 pre-scored carriers on 31 million-plus rows of data.

Who is Nivio Bind for?

Insurance brokers who place trucking coverage — they prospect carriers, build the submission, and onboard, then hand a complete, defensible risk package to the underwriter who prices and decides.

Is the Nivio Bind scoring a black box?

No. Every verdict is explainable and auditable — it shows the contributing signals behind each score, built for the regulatory and reinsurer conversations underwriters have to defend.

What systems does Nivio Bind integrate with?

Nivio Bind plugs into the policy-admin systems underwriters already use, including AIM, Duck Creek, Guidewire, and Sapiens.
