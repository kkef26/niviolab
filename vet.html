FAQ

Common questions

What is Nivio Vet?

Nivio Vet is carrier vetting software for freight brokers. It returns a full carrier vet — authority, insurance, CSA, nuclear-verdict flags, chameleon detection, and litigation — in 5 seconds, inline in the broker's TMS.

Why do freight brokers need carrier vetting?

Nuclear verdicts against brokers have crossed $100 million. Brokers vet 30 to 100 carriers a week, and one unsafe carrier on the book is a catastrophic liability. Nivio Vet makes vetting every carrier fast and defensible.

How fast is the Nivio Vet vet?

Under 5 seconds per carrier — fast enough to run on every load, not just the ones that feel risky — with an automatic, immutable audit trail.

What systems does Nivio Vet work with?

It integrates with broker TMS and vetting tools including McLeod, Revenova, Parade, Highway, RMIS, MyCarrierPortal, and Truckstop.
