# Nivio — SEO Keyword Map

Per-page keyword targeting. Primary keywords are assigned from domain expertise + search-intent analysis. **Volume/difficulty numbers still need live validation in Ahrefs (task #20) once the connector is stable** — the targeting below is sound, but confirm volumes before investing in any single term.

## How to read difficulty (my estimate)
- **Low** — niche, narrow intent, Nivio can rank within months of the domain going live
- **Med** — competitive but winnable with the page + a few supporting articles
- **High** — broad head term; treat as aspirational, win the long-tail first

## Per-page targets

### Home — `index.html`
- **Primary:** software for trucking and bulk commodity operators *(Low–Med)*
- **Secondary:** trucking operations software, bulk commodity software, operator-built trucking software, trucking workflow software
- **Intent:** brand/category discovery. The homepage isn't the main ranking workhorse — the product pages are. Optimize for brand + "what is Nivio."

### Dispatch — `dispatch.html` (lead horse)
- **Primary:** bulk fleet dispatch software *(Low–Med)*
- **Secondary:** tank truck dispatch software, bulk trucking dispatch optimization, fleet shift planning software, sand hauler dispatch software, crude hauling dispatch software
- **Long-tail to own fast (Low):** "dispatch software for sand haulers", "bulk fuel fleet dispatch", "HOS-compliant dispatch planning"
- **Intent:** high commercial — these searchers are evaluating a purchase. Highest priority for ranking.

### Marketer — `marketer.html`
- **Primary:** commodity marketing software *(Low)*
- **Secondary:** bulk commodity desk software, physical commodity scheduling software, commodity nomination software, crude marketing software
- **Note:** very niche — low volume but near-zero competition. Easy to own; expect small but high-intent traffic.

### Bind — `bind.html`
- **Primary:** trucking insurance submission software for brokers *(Med)*
- **Secondary:** carrier risk scoring, commercial trucking underwriting platform, carrier risk API, nuclear verdict risk scoring, MGA trucking underwriting
- **Intent:** high-value B2B. Pair with content on "carrier risk signals" and "nuclear verdict prevention."

### Carrier Shield — `carrier-shield.html`
- **Primary:** fleet insurance risk monitoring *(Low–Med)*
- **Secondary:** trucking insurance renewal software, carrier safety monitoring, ELD insurance data, fleet premium reduction software, CSA score monitoring
- **Intent:** fleet owners researching how to lower premiums — strong commercial intent.

### Vet — `vet.html`
- **Primary:** carrier vetting software *(Med)*
- **Secondary:** freight broker carrier vetting, carrier compliance check, carrier monitoring for brokers, nuclear verdict broker protection, carrier authority verification
- **Intent:** high — brokers actively shop for vetting tools after an E&O scare. Competitive (Highway, Carrier411, RMIS) — win the "nuclear verdict" + "5-second vet" angles they don't own.

### Board Room — `board-room.html`
- **Primary:** AI advisory board for SMBs *(Med)*
- **Secondary:** on-demand advisory board, AI decision-making tool for founders, virtual board of advisors, SMB decision support
- **Intent:** broad/horizontal. Different audience from the trucking pages — lower SEO priority for the July 1 launch.

## Cross-cutting recommendations
1. **No cannibalization** — each page owns a distinct primary keyword; the homepage targets brand/category, not product terms. Good.
2. **Supporting content** — each product page wants 2–3 supporting articles targeting its long-tail (this is the bridge to programmatic SEO, task #22).
3. **Validate before scaling spend** — before any paid or programmatic investment, confirm the primary-keyword volumes in Ahrefs. Niche B2B terms can have very low volume; the strategy may shift toward content + outbound if organic volume is thin.
