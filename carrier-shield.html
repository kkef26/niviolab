The modules

Your safety record, turned into leverage at renewal.

Renewal happens once a year. Your risk changes every day. Carrier Shield watches it continuously and hands you the proof when it counts.

01 · The mirror
See yourself the way your underwriter does.

Carrier Shield pulls the exact A–F verdict an underwriter would run on your DOT — the same nuclear-verdict, CSA, and litigation signals that decide your rate. No more being graded on data you've never seen. You walk into renewal already knowing your number.

02 · Renewal narrative
The binder builds itself — and finds your market.

Every quarter, Carrier Shield auto-generates a binder-ready renewal narrative from your live data — trends, dollar impact, peer position — then matches you to the insurers most likely to quote your profile, application pre-filled. Your broker just opens the link.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Nivio Carrier Shield?

Nivio Carrier Shield is a carrier-side insurance platform for fleet owners. It helps fleets find insurers, manage onboarding, and defend premiums by turning ELD, CSA, and claims data into a continuously updated, defensible renewal narrative.

How much can Nivio Carrier Shield save on premiums?

Pilot fleets average 18% premium savings at renewal by walking in with a data-backed risk-reduction story instead of being graded on the industry average.

Who is Nivio Carrier Shield for?

Fleet owners and their insurance risk teams who are safer than the industry average but pay like they are not — because their underwriter cannot see their real safety record.

What data does Nivio Carrier Shield use?

Continuous ELD, CSA, and claims data, integrated from Motive, Samsara, Geotab, and Omnitracs, benchmarked against the fleet's actual peer cohort.
